# Baby Toss

*Kid Tested, Mother Approved!*

A backyard game made into a browser game. Toss baby dolls into three trash cans from a set distance.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser (phone or computer). It loads three.js and cannon.js from cdnjs, so it needs an internet connection.

- Pick a grip, then press on the yard, pull down and let go, like a slingshot. Pull farther to throw farther; pull left to aim right.
- Keyboard: `1`–`7` pick a grip, arrow keys aim and set power, `Space` or `Enter` throws.

## Rules

- Three cans: the small front can is worth **2**, the big tall middle can **1**, and the very small bucket hidden behind the middle can **5**.
- A baby counts if its **body is in** the can; its head, arms and legs can stick out. A baby knocked in (or wriggling in) later in the same turn counts then.
- Each player tosses **3 babies** a turn.
- Pick the target score at the start (default **20**). You have to hit it **exactly**. Go over and you **bust**: your score goes back to what it was at the start of that turn, and your turn ends. Hitting it exactly ends your turn too.
- The round always finishes, so everyone gets the same number of turns. If more than one player is on the target at the end of a round, they play tiebreak turns until one scores the most.
- 1 to 12 players take turns in the order listed. No teams.

## Grips

The babies have a stuffed, floppy body with a hard vinyl head, arms and feet. Each grip throws differently:

| Grip | How it throws |
|---|---|
| Head | Steady, medium arc |
| Left / right hand | Cartwheels and curves left or right |
| Left / right foot | Big whip, flat and wild |
| Both feet | High lob, flips end over end |
| Overhand (body) | Flat and straight, most accurate |

## Settings

- **Living babies**: in the game the babies wiggle and cry. Turn it off and they're just dolls.
- **Sound**: on or off.

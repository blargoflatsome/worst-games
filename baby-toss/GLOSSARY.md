# Baby Toss

A backyard game: players toss baby dolls into three trash cans for points, racing to hit a target score exactly.

## The yard

**Can**:
One of the three targets. Each is worth a fixed number of points.
_Avoid_: Bin, trash can, target (target means the score)

**Front can**:
The small can closest to the thrower, worth 2.

**Tall can**:
The big, tall middle can, worth 1. The easiest to hit.
_Avoid_: Middle can, big can

**Back bucket**:
The very small can furthest away, partly hidden behind the tall can, worth 5.
_Avoid_: Back can, small can

**Throwing spot**:
The fixed place every toss starts from.
_Avoid_: Line, tee

## Babies and throwing

**Baby**:
A baby doll that gets tossed. A stuffed body with a hard vinyl head, arms and feet.
_Avoid_: Doll, ragdoll, player

**Living babies**:
The setting that lets babies move and cry as if alive. Off, they are only dolls.

**Toss**:
One baby thrown from the throwing spot.
_Avoid_: Throw, shot

**Grip**:
How the baby is held for a toss: head, left hand, right hand, left foot, right foot, both feet, or overhand by the
body.
_Avoid_: Hold, throw type

**Fully in**:
A baby whose every part is inside a can and below its rim. The only way a baby scores.
_Avoid_: In, made it

## Playing

**Side**:
Whoever owns a score: a player, or a team when teams are on.
_Avoid_: Team (when it may be one player)

**Turn**:
One player's three tosses.

**Round**:
Every side taking one turn. A round always finishes.

**Target**:
The score a side must reach exactly to win. Default 20.
_Avoid_: Goal, winning score

**Bust**:
A toss that would take a side over the target. The side's score goes back to what it was at the start of the turn.

**Tiebreak**:
Extra turns for sides tied on the target at the end of a round; most points in the turn wins.
_Avoid_: Overtime, sudden death

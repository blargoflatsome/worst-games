Pick a grip, then swipe up on the yard to toss. Longer swipe throws farther; angle it to aim.

Baby Toss

Kid Tested, Mother Approved!

Everyone gets 3 babies a turn. A baby only counts if it's fully in the can. Front can 2, tall can 1, the little bucket hiding at the back 5. Hit the target exactly: go over and you bust, back to your score from the start of that turn. The round always finishes, so everyone gets the same number of turns. Hold each baby by the head, a hand, a foot, both feet, or overhand by the body. Each throws differently.

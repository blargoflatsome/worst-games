Pick a grip, then press on the yard, pull down and let go. Pull farther to throw farther; pull left to aim right.

Baby Toss

Kid Tested, Mother Approved!

Everyone gets 3 babies a turn. A baby only counts if it's fully in the can. Front can 2, tall can 1, the little bucket hiding at the back 5. A baby knocked in later in the turn counts then. Hit the target exactly: go over and you bust, back to your score from the start of that turn. The round always finishes, so everyone gets the same number of turns. Hold each baby by the head, a hand, a foot, both feet, or overhand by the body. Each throws differently.
